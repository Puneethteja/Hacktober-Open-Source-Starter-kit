# Code of Conduct

This project is a place for beginners to learn. We want everyone to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background, or identity.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people asking basic questions
- Giving feedback about the work, not the person
- Accepting feedback gracefully and learning from mistakes
- Respecting different opinions and experiences

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Insults, mocking, or personal attacks
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private messages
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Spamming issues or pull requests

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that break this Code of Conduct, and may block people who repeatedly break it.

To report a problem, contact the GDSC NIT Rourkela team at [dscnitrourkela@gmail.com](mailto:dscnitrourkela@gmail.com). Reports are kept private.
